Headtorches on at the control gates, breath fogging. The climb is relentless and beautiful, switchback after switchback through moss and beech until the trees simply stop.

The ridge is the reason you come. Hours of running along the spine of a mountain with lakes on one side and a sea of peaks on the other.

Then the long valley: soft trail, tired feet, and the slow realisation that the last ten kilometres are always the longest ten kilometres.
