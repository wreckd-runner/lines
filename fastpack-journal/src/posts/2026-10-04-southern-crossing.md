We left the road end in drizzle with packs light enough to run with — a quilt, a stove, two days of food and not much else. The first hours are all climbing through forest, the kind where the track is more root than ground.

## Up into the white

Above the bushline the cloud came down to meet us. The ridge became a narrow strip of tussock with the world deleted on both sides, poles planted every few metres like punctuation. You run by feel up there: the wind tells you which side the drop is on.

> "Is that the hut or another rock?"

It was another rock. Several times.

## Day two

Clear morning, frost on the tussock, the whole range laid out in folds to the south. The descent is long and kind to tired legs until it isn't, and then it's just about getting to the car before the light goes.
