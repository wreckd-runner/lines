# About

This is a journal of running and fastpacking trips in Aotearoa — light packs, long days, and the lines they leave on the map.

Every story is tied to the GPX track it came from. The black relief behind each line is drawn from Toitū Te Whenua LINZ’s national elevation model: 1&nbsp;m LiDAR where it’s been flown, the 8&nbsp;m contour model where it hasn’t.

Stories here come from us and from you. If you’ve been somewhere worth writing about, [send it in](../submit/).
